# Red panda test plan (0.1.79)

The first slice of the Treetop trio (Austin's card, 2026-10-16) is the Red Panda (spec 5.2: "red_panda | Red panda | Elevated travel and resting | Continuous climbing routes, nest boxes"). Red pandas climb like raccoons, so their exhibit has to be climb-proof, but they live up in the trees: they travel along the canopy at dawn and dusk, drape over a branch through the day and sleep curled up at night, on the canopy or in a Nest Box (a new block). In a hot biome they need shade. The model is the new blocky style (Austin: "This is minecraft remember"). This build adds new animals, a new block and textures, so fully close and reopen Minecraft after the build.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Small Carriers, a Basic Feeder, Leafy Rations, glass blocks, oak saplings and bone meal (or logs and leaves to build trees), planks and sticks for Nest Boxes, a fence. A park day is 20 minutes of play; `/zoocraft:skip 1200` runs one park day now; `/time set` changes the time of day (0 sunrise, 6000 noon, 12000 sunset, 18000 midnight). Wild red pandas live in bamboo jungles, one or two together.

| 1 | `/summon zoocraft:wild_red_panda` a few times in Creative. | 3 | By day (`/time set 6000`), watch one up in a tree. | It lies draped over the edge of the canopy, the legs and tail hanging down, eyes shut. |
| 4 | At night (`/time set 15000`), watch one. | It sleeps curled into a ball with the tail over the face. Around midnight (`/time set 18000`) it's up and about for a while. |
| 5 | In Survival, walk toward a wild one from about 10 blocks. | Within about 8 blocks it heads up a tree. |
| 6 | In Survival, walk right up to one on the ground (within 2 blocks). | It stands up on its hind legs with its arms raised and hisses. No damage. It does this at most every 15 seconds. In Creative it doesn't. |
| 7 | Hold a Leafy Ration near a wild one. | It comes to you. Catch it with a Small Carrier. |
| 8 | Craft a Nest Box at the Zoo Workshop (planks and sticks) and place it facing different ways. | A small wooden box with a round hole in the front and a little roof; the hole faces you when you place it. You can walk through it. Use it: "Nest Box: not in an exhibit...". |
| 9 | Build a glass-walled exhibit about 9 x 9, 5 or more high, with two oak trees whose leaves touch, back from the walls. Put a Nest Box on the floor and another up in a tree level with the top of the leaves, right beside them. Add a Basic Feeder with Leafy Rations. Measure it and release two red pandas. Open the Clipboard. | The exhibit measures, climb-proof. "Canopy: N of 12 blocks up in the trees, reached from a trunk" and "Nest Boxes: 2 of 2". The equipment line counts 2 nest boxes. | 10 | Build the same with fences instead of glass. | Measuring says they could climb out, as for raccoons. Release one anyway (or break a glass block): it climbs out over the fence along the way out. |
| 11 | Take away one tree (or both) and measure. | With fewer than 6 canopy blocks for each grown one: the warning "Red Pandas travel and rest up in the trees: they need 12 canopy blocks..." and their comfort stops at 55 (the advice names more canopy). Leaves with no trunk to climb don't count. |
| 12 | Break the Nest Boxes and measure. | "Red Pandas sleep in Nest Boxes: place one for each grown one (0 of 2)..." and comfort capped at 55. |
| 13 | Watch them through a park day. | Dawn and dusk: up the trees, along the canopy, sat up at the feeder chewing a stalk of bamboo held in a paw, grooming. Getting into the box up in the tree, it walks from the canopy straight into it; it comes out the same way. |
| 14 | Build an exhibit like step 9 in a desert or savanna with little shade. By day they sprawl and pant instead of draping. Add leaves or a roof over a third of the floor and measure: it clears. |
| 15 | Turn breeding on with a grown pair and skip park days until cubs come. | 2 or 3 small buff-grey cubs. Their first park day they stay in a Nest Box (you hear them twitter); then they follow, play and climb. They turn red when grown. |
