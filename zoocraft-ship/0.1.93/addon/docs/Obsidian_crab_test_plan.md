# Obsidian Crab test plan (0.1.93)

The last of the End trio is the Obsidian Crab (spec 5.4: "Low armored black shell | Scrapes minerals and digs | Rocky cave, mineral feeder, substrate"). It is built on the coconut crab and drawn as a true crab: a low, wide black shell and a sideways walk. It scrapes minerals off rock with its claws and digs itself in backwards to rest. This version adds new entities, a new block (the Mineral Feeder), textures and a particle, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

You need:
- a superflat world and a Wild Obsidian Crab Spawn Egg;
- a stack of end stone, a few obsidian, an end stone brick slab, a fence, and a chorus plant (or chorus flowers to grow one);
- 10 polished blackstone, 12 gravel, 3 nether quartz, bone meal, and basalt or blackstone;
- a Zoo Workshop, a Stabilized Carrier (Dimensional Preparation research), a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard and a Field Journal;
- a Water Trough and a water bucket, and glass for the walls and roof.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In Creative, use the spawn egg 3 or 4 times on the flat field. Look at them from all sides, close up, then in the dark. | A low, wide crab about as big as a coconut crab: its shell under a quarter of a block high, its legs reaching nearly a block across. It has four legs a side, two eyes on short stalks and two antennae at the front, and a big left claw and a smaller right one. Most are Obsidian: glossy black with a violet sheen along the shell's ridges and violet claw tips. Now and then one is Crying: black flecked with glowing violet. The eyes glow violet in the dark. A male is bigger; a female is about 0.7 of his size. |
| 2 | Stay in Creative and watch one for 3 or 4 minutes. | It walks sideways, its body across the way it goes, about a block a second, turning to lead with whichever side means less turning. For the last block to something it turns and walks forward, slower. It stops to scrape the ground with its claw tips, bringing each claw to its mouth by turns, with dark grit flying off. It also sifts the grass a little, half sunk in, and stands up on its legs with its eyes up and antennae flicking. Every 2 to 3 minutes it digs itself in backwards where it is, over 3 seconds, throwing up grit. It stays dug in 30 to 60 seconds with only its eyes and big claw showing, the claw across the hole. Then it digs out and goes on. |
| 3 | Put two of them a block apart. | Both rear with their claws up for 2 seconds, clacking. Then the smaller one (a female, or either of two alike) sidles off a few blocks. The same two don't face off again for about 30 seconds. They never end up on top of each other. |
| 4 | Put down some end stone near them: single blocks, a stack of 2, a few obsidian standing on the ground, the slab, the fence and the chorus plant. | A crab walks up the face of a single block and over, and down off the far side. It never climbs the stack of 2, and never gets onto the slab, the fence top or the chorus plant. With rock beside the ground it stands on, it goes to scrape the rock face, facing it, instead of the ground. |
| 5 | Switch to Survival and walk up to one, not sneaking. | Within 4 blocks it rears at you with its claws up and open, clacking. Within 2 blocks it bolts sideways away from you, fast (3 blocks a second), 4 to 8 blocks, then digs itself in. It stays in 10 to 20 seconds, then comes out. |
| 6 | Sneak up to another one and stand still beside it. Then punch it. | Sneaking, it lets you come right up, and it doesn't mind you standing there. The punch makes it bolt and dig in. It takes no damage from a fall. |
| 7 | Make Mineral Pellets (basalt or blackstone with bone meal makes 2). Stand 8 or 9 blocks from one and hold them. | It turns and walks over to you forward, antennae flicking, stops about a block from you and reaches up at the pellets with its claws, for as long as you hold them. It doesn't rear at you while you hold them. Put them away and it goes back to what it was doing. |
| 8 | Go to the End's outer islands (through an End gateway). | Wild Obsidian Crabs, alone or 2 together, on the end stone. They walk sideways, scrape the rock and look about. They rest dug in at the foot of a rock (an obsidian pillar, a step of end stone), going back to the same burrow when they're near it. Herd one toward an island's edge: it never walks off into the void. |
| 9 | Open the Field Journal on the Obsidian Crab and do its tasks. | Tasks: spot one; watch one scrape minerals off a rock with its claws; watch one dig itself in backwards; come at one in Survival and see it rear with its claws up (two facing off counts too). The care notes cover the rocky cave (a roof and rock faces), Mineral Substrate, the Mineral Feeder, their bouts and burrows, face-offs and breeding. |
| 10 | At the Zoo Workshop, make a Mineral Feeder: 5 polished blackstone in a U (like a cauldron). Make Mineral Substrate: 4 gravel, a nether quartz and bone meal make 4. Do that twice. | A Mineral Feeder: a low, wide basin of polished blackstone. And 8 Mineral Substrate. |
| 11 | Build an exhibit for 3 crabs. It needs glass walls, a floor of at least 24 blocks (12 for one, 6 more for each extra), 2 blocks of air over it, and a roof over nearly all of it (at least 9 in 10 blocks). Put in 4 or more end stone and 6 or more Mineral Substrate in the floor. Build rock for 8 or more faces: a wall of obsidian 2 high along one side, a block or two of obsidian standing on the floor, or a step of end stone. Place the Mineral Feeder, put Mineral Pellets in it, and add a Water Trough with water. Look at the feeder as it fills. Measure the exhibit with the Habitat Marker and open the Clipboard. | The feeder shows a heap of pale pellets, fuller as you add more. The Clipboard says "Rock to scrape: N of 8 faces (...)" and "Mineral Substrate: N of 6 blocks to dig their burrows in". There are no care warnings about the roof, rock or substrate. |
| 12 | With the Field Journal done, use the Stabilized Carrier on a wild crab with Mineral Pellets in your inventory. Release it in the exhibit. Do the same for 2 more, then stand outside in Creative and watch for a few minutes. | They walk sideways about the floor, never up onto the feeder or the trough. They scrape the rock faces, eat at the Mineral Feeder facing it, sift the substrate and look about. Now and then two face off. At the end of a bout each walks to the Mineral Substrate (a block beside the rock first) and digs itself in backwards. Next time it goes back to the same block while that one is free. Two never dig into one block, and they never end up on top of each other. |
| 13 | Put a Basic Feeder with Mineral Pellets in a Quartz Beetle, Shimmer Snail or Basalt Tortoise exhibit, and a Mineral Feeder in another. | Those animals eat from both. The crabs eat only from the Mineral Feeder. |
| 14 | Switch to Survival, step inside the crab exhibit and walk up to one. | Within 3 blocks it rears. Within 1.5 blocks it scuttles to its burrow and digs in. |
| 15 | Break the rock until fewer than 8 faces are left and measure. Then dig out Mineral Substrate until there are 5 in the floor, and measure again. | Two care warnings: "Obsidian Crabs scrape the minerals off rock with their claws: 8 or more rock faces at the floor's level (N now; comfort can't rise above 50 without them)...", and "Obsidian Crabs dig themselves into Mineral Substrate to rest: 6 or more blocks in the floor (5 now ...". A crab that finds no free substrate block rests pressed flat by a rock, eyes folded in. Put both back and measure: the warnings go. |
| 16 | Turn breeding on and wait (or skip park days). | A female carries 2 or 3 eggs under her tail; the message says so. In 3 park days the crablets come out from under her. They are about a third of a male's size (half a female's), each with a little spiral shell on its back. They keep near her, scrape and sift, and dig in when she rests. In 5 park days they're grown. |
| 17 | Check a Shimmer Snail, End Hopper, Quartz Beetle or Basalt Tortoise exhibit you have. | They behave as before, and still eat Mineral Pellets from a Basic Feeder. |
| 18 | Look at the content log after the restart. | No script or content errors. |
