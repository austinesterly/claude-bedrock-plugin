## 0.1.113: drawn the red panda's way

Austin (2026-10-26, on 0.1.111, with photos of a crocodile and a zebra): "Eyes are messed up here. Zebras also look bad. Look at how the red panda is made, that is a perfect example of how to go from real life to minecraft". The red panda's skin is a few flat colours set out by rule, its tail rings a texel each, its face drawn texel by texel, its snout a box of its own. The five are now painted the same way: no more mottled noise. The crocodile's eyes are bumps on top of its head, yellow with a black slit on the outer side (they were flat yellow dashes, like goggles), and its teeth a sparse row. The zebra's stripes are even bands, two texels black and two white, upright round the barrel, level on the rump and legs, across the neck, mane and face, with a white belly and a dark brown muzzle. The lion and tiger have a snout box with the nose and mouth drawn on it, amber eyes with pupils, and flat coats; the tiger's stripes are clean bands that stop at lengths of their own. The raccoon's coat is flat grey with pale flecks, its tail rings clean, its eyes a glint in the mask. The legs of the cats and the zebra are a texel thicker. A full restart of Minecraft, then:
