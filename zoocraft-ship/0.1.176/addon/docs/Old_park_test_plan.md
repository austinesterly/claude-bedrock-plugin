# The abandoned park: test plan (0.1.176)

A new way to start: an old, abandoned wildlife park laid into the ground round you, which you bring back to life with
the tutorial. It has a broken Aluminum Fence (6 breaks), an overgrown Park Entrance Gate, a caretaker's cabin with a
Noticeboard by its door, the shell of an old workshop, the park stores, an old gravel path with gaps, a viewing deck and
an unfinished Rabbit Meadow pen. The stores and the workshop's barrel hold what the tutorial's first crafts need.

Two ways to get one:
- The Park Deed, if you don't own a park yet: choose "Start from an old park", then "Build it". It's built round where
  you stand and it's yours. Once a player a world.
- The command (operators, cheats on): `/zoocraft:oldpark` builds one with no owner round you, `/zoocraft:oldpark x z`
  at x z. From the server console with no numbers, round the world spawn, and the spawn moves to just outside its gate
  (this is how the starter world will get its park). Its Noticeboard then offers it to the first player who wants it.

It needs fairly level, dry ground in the Overworld, at least 44 blocks from another park's Park Deed spot. Trees and
blocks where the yard and the pen go are cleared; the rest keeps its own ground, and the fence follows the ground.

New block (the Noticeboard): quit Minecraft completely and start it again. Works in any world.
