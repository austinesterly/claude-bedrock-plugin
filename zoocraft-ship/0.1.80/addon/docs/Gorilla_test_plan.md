# Gorilla test plan (0.1.80)

The second slice of the Treetop trio (Austin's card, 2026-10-16) is the Gorilla (spec 5.2: "gorilla | Gorilla | Social interaction and puzzle solving | Strong fixtures and climbing structures"). Gorillas live in a troop, one silverback with his females and young. They are dangerous like the big cats (Holding Dens, a double Keeper Gate, crated from the den), and their exhibit needs strong walls: they climb trees, Climbing Frames and fences and pull themselves up 3.5 blocks, and they can't swim. A wild silverback bluffs (the card's recommended answer, "Bluff"): he warns you off, charges and stops short, and only means it if you keep coming or hurt his troop. The model is the new blocky style. This build adds new animals and textures, so fully close and reopen Minecraft after the build.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Reinforced Crates, Fruit Rations and Leafy Rations, a Basic Feeder and a Puzzle Feeder, a Climbing Frame, stone bricks (or glass) for walls, oak fences, Holding Dens and two Keeper Gates. A park day is 20 minutes of play; `/zoocraft:skip 1200` runs one park day now; `/time set` changes the time of day (0 sunrise, 6000 noon, 12000 sunset, 18000 midnight). Wild gorillas live in jungles (not bamboo jungles), 2 to 4 together.

| 2 | Watch a wild troop through a day (`/time set 1000`, `6000`, `9000`, `12000`, `18000`). | Morning and afternoon they feed: sat up with food in a hand, or reaching into the leaves of a bush or a low branch and pulling a stem down. At dusk each sits and pulls branches in round it (building a nest), then sleeps on its side through the night. Females now and then climb a trunk and sit on top; the silverback stays on the ground. |
| 3 | Watch the silverback a while. | Now and then the display: he hoots (rising notes), stands up and drums on his chest with both hands, drops onto all fours and slaps the ground. |
| 4 | In Survival, walk toward a wild troop. | Within about 10 blocks the silverback turns to you and gives the display ("A silverback is warning you off"). Stay further than 6 blocks: he stands his ground beating his chest, then lets you be. |
| 5 | Walk on to within 6 blocks during the display. | He charges with a roar and stops short (2 to 3 blocks off), then stands beating his chest. No damage. |
| 6 | Keep coming (within 3 blocks), or hit one of the troop. | The charge is real: he runs right at you and shoves (3 hearts and a push back), then lets you be a while. In Creative and on Peaceful none of this happens. |
| 7 | Throw a Fruit Ration down near a wild one (drop it from your hand). | It walks to it, sits and eats. While it eats, use a Reinforced Crate on it: it goes in. Before it has eaten, the crate says "Too dangerous to crate: throw a Fruit Ration down near the gorilla and crate it while it eats". |
| 8 | Build an exhibit about 15 x 15 with walls of stone bricks 3 blocks high, a Holding Den for each gorilla, a double Keeper Gate, a Basic Feeder with Leafy Rations and a Puzzle Feeder with Fruit Rations. | 9 | Make the walls 4 high (stone bricks or glass) and measure. | "Strong walls: yes, gorillas can't get out". Release a silverback and two females. |

Leave it while gorillas live there: one now and then climbs out along that way and every park member is told where. It's loose: catch it as a wild one (step 7).
