# Piglins, piglin brutes and zombified piglins: test plan (0.1.206)

The game's piglin, piglin brute and zombified piglin as zoo mobs, caught and kept the zombie's way. They look like the
game's own (the same models and skins, the weapon in hand) and make the game's sounds. In the zoo they never attack,
never turn into zombified piglins, never burn in the sun and never despawn.

Each keeps the weapon it held in the wild: a piglin's golden sword, crossbow or golden spear, a zombified piglin's
golden sword, golden spear, crossbow or golden axe (a piglin or brute that turned keeps its own), a brute's golden axe.
A piglin or zombified piglin also keeps any golden armour it wore. They never use their weapons in the zoo.

A piglin (calmed or in the zoo) still barters: use a gold ingot on it and it holds the ingot up, looks it over for 8
seconds and tosses you something from the game's own bartering list (soul sand, a fire charge, blackstone and so on).
While it looks one over it takes no other. Brutes and zombified piglins never take gold.

In the wild: piglins in the Nether's crimson forests and wastes and in bastion remnants; brutes only in bastion
remnants (no new ones ever come); zombified piglins in the Nether wastes, crimson forests and fortresses, and in the
Overworld beside Nether portals, from a pig lightning strikes and from a piglin or brute taken out of the Nether.
Out of the Nether a piglin or brute shakes and turns into a zombified piglin after 15 seconds, so calm it in the Nether
(or calm the zombified piglin it turns into: it keeps the weapon and armour).

How to catch one (Mob Taming research): fill in the Field Journal first (spot one, then see one calmed: keep your eyes
on it for a few seconds). Hit it till it has a quarter of its health or less (a piglin 4 of 16, a brute 12 of 50, a
zombified piglin 5 of 20), then use a Passivity Serum on it, looking at it up close. Hit a piglin and every piglin and
brute near it comes for you; hit a zombified piglin and every one near it does. Then, with a Meat Ration in your
inventory, use an empty Mob Cage on it, and use the cage on the floor inside its pen. Or hold a Meat Ration: it follows
you.

What they want (a pen beside a Mob House, glass to the hall): at least 5 by 5 of floor for the first (10 more blocks
for each more); Meat Rations from a Basic Feeder. Piglins: a bastion's or crimson forest's ground (blackstone of any
kind, gilded blackstone or crimson nylium) under half the floor or more, and a block of gold or more in the pen.
Brutes: a bastion's floor (blackstone of any kind or gilded blackstone). Zombified piglins: the Nether wastes' ground
(netherrack, nether gold ore, nether quartz ore or crimson nylium). Without them comfort can't rise above 55, and the
Exhibit Clipboard says why. Piglins and brutes can share a pen (the Bastion); zombified piglins keep one to
themselves. They are never bred: catch more.

New mobs: quit Minecraft completely and start it again. Works in any world.
