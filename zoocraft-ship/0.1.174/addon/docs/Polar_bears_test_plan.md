# Polar bears: test plan (0.1.174)

The game's polar bear as a zoo animal. It looks and moves like the game's own (the same model and skin; a cub is a
small copy of its mother with a bigger head). It is dangerous, so it is kept like the lions and tigers: a Holding Den
for each grown bear, a double Keeper Gate, and keepers go in only when every bear is shut in.

How to catch one: find a wild polar bear (snowy plains, ice spikes, frozen oceans). Throw a Fish Ration, a raw cod or a
raw salmon on the ground near a grown one. It walks over and eats it; while it eats (and for a little while after),
use a Reinforced Crate on it. Cubs stay with their mothers and don't come to fish. Hit a wild bear and it stops
coming.

What a zoo polar bear wants: a big exhibit (220 blocks of floor for one, 80 more for each more, 4 blocks of headroom), a
pool of at least a seventh of the floor with 8 spots 2 deep to swim and 4 spots 1 deep to wade, half the floor dry,
snow or ice under a quarter of the floor, a stocked Cooling Unit, and Fish Rations or raw cod or salmon (a Basic
Feeder). The numbers are Manitoba's Polar Bear Protection Regulation, the strictest polar bear law there is, scaled to
blocks.

In its exhibit: it wanders the bank, swims across the pool, and now and then stands up on its hind legs to look about.
Go into its exhibit in Survival and a grown one stands up facing you (the warning). Stay within 5 blocks for 3 seconds
(6 if you sneak), or walk right up to it, and it drops down, charges, rears up over you and comes down on you with its
paws, then backs off. Stay further than 5 blocks off and it only keeps warning you; leave and it calms down.

A new animal: quit Minecraft completely and start it again. Works in any world.
