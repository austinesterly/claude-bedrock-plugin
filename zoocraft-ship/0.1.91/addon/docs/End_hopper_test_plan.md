# End Hopper test plan (0.1.91)

The first of the End trio is the End Hopper (spec 5.4: "end_hopper | Small creature with long angular legs | High jumps between platforms | Jump clearance, roof, landing pads"). It is built on the springhare, life-size (about a rabbit), with a jerboa's long angular hind legs and its zigzag escape. It leaps up onto blocks in the wild and between Landing Pads in a zoo, a new block. This version adds new entities, a new block and textures, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

Needs: a superflat world. A Wild End Hopper Spawn Egg, chorus fruit (3 make 4 Chorus Browse), a stack of end stone, 6 purple wool, a Zoo Workshop, a Stabilized Carrier (Dimensional Preparation research), a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, a Basic Feeder, a Water Trough and a water bucket, glass for the walls and the roof, and a few blocks for pillars.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In Creative, use the spawn egg 3 or 4 times on the flat field. Look at them from all sides, close up. | A blocky animal the size of a rabbit sitting up. It has long angular hind legs (a thick thigh, a thin shank slanting back from a sharp knee, a long foot), short front paws, tall ears and a long tail ending in a dark brush. Its big dark eyes have a violet shine, and faint violet patches on its back and flanks glow in the dark. Most are Pale (end-stone cream, lilac inside the ears, a deep purple brush); now and then one is Purpur (lilac-grey, a near-black brush). |
| 2 | Stay in Creative and watch one for 3 or 4 minutes. | It hops about on its hind legs. Now and then it crouches, leaps 2 to 4 blocks across the field in a smooth arc, lands with all four feet together and sits up with its ears up. It forages hunched on all fours, sits up to look round, and sometimes sits with its head and ears flat on the ground, listening, for a few seconds. Every minute or so it naps for 20 to 40 seconds, standing asleep with its head and front paws tucked between its hind legs and its tail round its feet. It never stays still for long. |
| 3 | Put down some end stone near it: single blocks, a stack of 2 and a stack of 3, with open air above. Add a slab, a fence and a chorus plant. | It leaps up onto the blocks 1 to 3 high and down off them again. It never lands on the slab, the fence top or the chorus plant. |
| 4 | Switch to Survival and walk at one. | Within about 6 blocks it gets away: 3 to 5 quick, low darts, each turned left or right of the way away from you, by turns. Then it sits bolt upright, watching you. Walk a block or two nearer and it goes again. If you walk after it, it draws away from you. If you sprint, it about keeps its distance. You can't catch one by running. |
| 5 | Sneak up to one. Then stand up and hold Chorus Browse. | Sneaking, it lets you come. Holding Chorus Browse within 10 blocks, one hops over, sits up a block and a half in front of you and sniffs, for as long as you hold it. It doesn't run from you while you hold it. |
| 6 | Go to the End's outer islands (through an End gateway). | End Hoppers 1 to 3 together on the end stone, leaping up onto the rises and down off them. Chase one to an island's edge: it never leaps out over the void. With the void ahead, it doubles back past you to one side. |
| 7 | Open the Field Journal on the End Hopper and do its tasks. | Tasks: spot one; watch one leap (onto a Landing Pad, or up onto a block in the wild); come close to a wild one in Survival and watch it zigzag away; watch one rest its head and ears on the ground, listening. The care notes cover a roof, Landing Pads, end stone and Chorus Browse. |
| 8 | At the Zoo Workshop, make Landing Pads: 3 purple wool over 3 end stone. Place one, walk on it and break it. | 3 Landing Pads: a full block with end-stone-brick sides and a quilted purple cushion on top, with a pale ring in the middle. Wool's sounds on top. A broken one drops itself. |
| 9 | Build an exhibit for 3 hoppers. Use glass walls and a full glass roof with 5 blocks of air between the floor and the roof, and at least 52 blocks of floor (36 for one, 8 more for each extra). Put 8 or more end stone in the floor, plus a Basic Feeder with Chorus Browse and a Water Trough with water. Place 3 Landing Pads within a leap of the floor or of each other (3 up and 5 across at most), each with 3 blocks of air over it. Put one of them on a 1-block pillar so its top is 2 over the floor. Measure it with the Habitat Marker and open the Clipboard. | "Roof: closed (they can't leap out)" and "Landing Pads: 3 of 3 counting, 1 high (2 or more blocks over the floor)". No care warnings about pads. |
| 10 | With the Field Journal done, use the Stabilized Carrier on a wild hopper with Chorus Browse in your inventory. Release it in the exhibit. Do the same for 2 more, then stand outside in Creative and watch for a few minutes. | Pad runs: it crouches, leaps to a free pad, lands, sits up 1 to 3 seconds, then goes on to another, 2 to 5 leaps in a run, and sometimes back down to the floor. Two hoppers are never on one pad: one waits or goes elsewhere. Walking about the floor, they go round the pads, not over them. They forage at the feeder, listen, sit up and nap as in step 2. |
| 11 | Switch to Survival and step inside, walking up to one. | Within 3 blocks it leaps at once to the pad farthest from you. Take the pads out and try again: it zigzags across the floor instead. It never leaves the exhibit. |
| 12 | Break pads until there are 2, and measure again. Then put a third back on the floor, but not the high one, and measure. | With 2: a care warning, "End Hoppers leap between Landing Pads: 3 or more that count (2 now) ...", and comfort stays at 50 or below. With 3 but none high: "End Hoppers want a high leap: put at least one Landing Pad 2 or more blocks over the floor ...". Put the high one back: the warnings go. |
| 13 | Put a block 2 above one pad (one block of air between them) and measure. | The Clipboard counts it out: "1 without 3 blocks of air over them", and the pads warning comes back. Take the block away. |
| 14 | Break 2 or 3 roof blocks and measure. | "Roof: open: they could leap out", and a safety warning: "End Hoppers leap 3 blocks high: the air leads out of the exhibit near x y z ...". A new hopper won't move in until it's roofed over again. |
| 15 | Turn breeding on and wait (or skip park days). | One pup, half size. It keeps to the floor by its mother in short hops and never leaps. It doesn't run from you. In 4 park days it's grown and leaps like the others. |
| 16 | Check a Chorus Deer, Void Ray or Rift Ferret exhibit you have, and a moth or beetle exhibit. | They behave as before. |
| 17 | Look at the content log after the restart. | No script or content errors. |
