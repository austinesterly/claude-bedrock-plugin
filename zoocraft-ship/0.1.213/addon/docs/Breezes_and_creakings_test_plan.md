# Breezes and creakings: test plan (0.1.213)

The game's breeze and creaking as zoo mobs in Mob House pens, caught and kept the zombie's way. Each looks and sounds
like the game's own (the same models, skins and animations, and the game's sounds). In the zoo a breeze never leaps at
anyone or fires a wind charge, and a creaking never stalks anyone, freezes when you look at it or crumbles away at dawn.
Neither despawns.

In the wild breezes come only from the trial spawners in trial chambers (set in chiseled tuff in the combat rooms).
Creakings come only from an active creaking heart (one set between two pale oak logs) at night in a pale garden; while
its heart stands, nothing hurts that creaking, and it goes at dawn. In Creative a spawn egg makes either (a creaking from
a spawn egg stays and dies to a single hit).

How to catch one (Mob Taming research): fill in the Field Journal first (spot one, then see one calmed: keep your eyes
on it for a few seconds; a zoo one counts). A breeze: weaken it to a quarter of its health or less (7.5 of 30), look at
it up close and use a Passivity Serum. A creaking needs no weakening (it has 1 health): just use the serum on it, even a
heart's one. It sparkles green, calms down and stands dazed for a moment. Then, with a wind charge (a breeze) or a resin
clump (a creaking) in your inventory, use an empty Mob Cage on it, and use the cage on the floor inside its pen. A calmed
one also follows you while you hold its food. Careful with a calmed creaking: a single hit still kills it.

What they want (a pen beside a Mob House, a roof over it, at least 5 by 5 of floor for the first and 10 more blocks
each, 3 blocks of headroom):
- Breezes: a trial chamber's floor (tuff, polished tuff, tuff bricks or chiseled tuff) under half the floor or more;
  glass to the hall; wind charges in a Basic Feeder (a breeze rod counts as 4).
- Creakings: a pale garden's floor (pale moss blocks or pale oak planks) under half the floor or more, and the dark: no
  daylight in the pen and nothing brighter than light 7 (tinted glass to the hall, Nocturnal Lamps for the guests to see
  them by); resin clumps in a Basic Feeder (a block of resin counts as 9).
- Without the floor comfort can't rise above 55, and the Exhibit Clipboard says why. A Water Trough each.
- One is happy alone; two share a pen well. Breezes and creakings each keep a pen to themselves.

They are never bred (the game's have no young): catch more.

New mobs: quit Minecraft completely and start it again. Works in any world.
