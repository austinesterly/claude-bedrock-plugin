# Tiger test plan (0.1.76)

The second slice of Big predators (Austin's card, 2026-10-15) is the Tiger (spec 5.2: "tiger | Tiger | Solitary patrolling and swimming | Privacy and separate adult territories"). Tigers are dangerous like lions: inside their exhibit a loose tiger warns a player off (the threat face: ears twisted round, a snarl, a hiss), then charges and swipes ("Warn, then swipe"), and keepers go in only through the double Keeper Gate with every tiger shut in a Holding Den. Tigers live alone: only one grown tiger (or a tigress with her cubs) is out at a time, the others shut in their dens, taking turns a park day each. A pair meets only on an introduction day, and only that pair breeds. They want screened floor to get out of sight, a pool to swim in and shallows to lie in. In the wild a tiger ambushes people: it creeps up while their back is turned, freezes when they look, and rushes and pounces from close (Austin: "Also make them very dangerous in the wild"). This build adds a new animal and textures, so fully close and reopen Minecraft after the build.

Needs: the 0.1.75 setup (a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Reinforced Crates, 2 Keeper Gates, Holding Dens, a Basic Feeder, Meat Rations, a Scent Post), water buckets, stone, leaves or saplings, a few logs, glass, fence blocks. A park day is 20 minutes of play; `/zoocraft:skip 1200` runs one park day now; `/time set` changes the time of day. Wild Bengal tigers live in jungles and wild Amur tigers in cold taigas, alone and rare. Keep your health up between the danger steps (`/effect @s instant_health`), and turn the difficulty to Easy or harder.
