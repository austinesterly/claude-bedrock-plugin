# Guardians and elder guardians: test plan (0.1.212)

The game's guardian and elder guardian as zoo mobs in a Mob House water pen, caught and kept the zombie's way. Each
looks and sounds like the game's own (the same model, skins and animations, the elder the big pale one, and the game's
sounds) and swims at the game's own pace. In the zoo they never fire their lasers, an elder never puts Mining Fatigue on
anyone, and they never despawn. Guardians and elder guardians may share a pen (the Ocean Monument).

In the wild guardians live only at ocean monuments, in the water in and around them. Elder guardians: only three in
each monument, made with it and never again (one in the top room, one in each wing). In Creative a spawn egg makes one.

How to catch one (Mob Taming research): fill in the Field Journal first (spot one, then see one calmed: keep your eyes
on it for a few seconds; a zoo one counts). Weaken it to a quarter of its health or less (7.5 of 30; an elder 20 of
80), look at it up close and use a Passivity Serum: it sparkles green, calms down and stands dazed for a moment. Then,
with a Fish Ration in your inventory, use an empty Mob Cage on it, and use the cage on the bank inside its pen, beside
the water: it goes into the water. A calmed one also follows you while you hold a Fish Ration.

What they want (a water pen beside a Mob House hall: a tank shut in all round with a roof, walled off from the hall with
glass, at least 25 blocks of water for the first and 10 more each, all of them 2 deep or more, and a bank beside the water
inside to use the Habitat Marker on, 2 blocks of headroom over it):
- An ocean monument's floor (prismarine, prismarine bricks, dark prismarine or sea lanterns) under half the pen or more
  (the bottom of the water and the bank); without it comfort can't rise above 55, and the Exhibit Clipboard says why.
- Fish Rations in an Aquarium Feeder over the water. Unlike the drowned they don't need the dark.
- One is happy alone; up to six guardians share a pen well, and two elders. Guardians and elders may share one pen.

They are never bred (the game's guardians have no young): catch more.

New mobs: quit Minecraft completely and start it again. Works in any world.
