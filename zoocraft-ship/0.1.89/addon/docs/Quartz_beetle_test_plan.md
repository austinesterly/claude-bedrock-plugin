# Quartz Beetle test plan (0.1.89)

The second of the Nether pair is the Quartz Beetle (spec 5.3: "quartz_beetle | Chunky beetle with quartz plates | Burrows through mineral substrate | Heated cave, mineral bed"). It is a fantasy rhinoceros beetle, drawn big so it's easy to see (Austin's card "Big beetle": about 0.6 blocks long). By day it lies buried in a new block, Mineral Substrate, with only its quartz-plated back showing; at night it digs out, eats, and two males that meet wrestle. Its young are grubs that live in the substrate. This version adds new entities, a new block and textures, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Thermal Carriers (Heat Systems research), a Thermal Regulator with fuel, a Water Trough and a water bucket, a Basic Feeder, Mineral Pellets, 8 gravel, 2 nether quartz, 2 bone meal, and stone bricks or blackstone for the walls and the roof. A park day is 20 minutes of game time.
