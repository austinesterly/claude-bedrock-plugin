# Chorus Moth test plan (0.1.94)

The first of the End pair is the Chorus Moth (spec 5.4: "chorus_moth | Purple angular wings | Hovers around chorus flowers | Flight space, chorus nectar, low light"). It is a fantasy hawk moth built on the elephant hawk-moth, drawn big so it's easy to see: half a block from wing tip to wing tip. It works its way round chorus flowers, hovering at each to sip, and shivers its wings warm before every flight. This version adds new entities, textures and a recipe, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

You need:
- a superflat world and a Wild Chorus Moth Spawn Egg;
- a stack of end stone, end stone bricks for the walls and roof, 6 or more chorus flowers and a few chorus plants (from the Creative inventory: a chorus plant stands only on end stone or another chorus plant, and a chorus flower only on end stone or a chorus plant);
- sugar and chorus fruit, a glass pane, two cornflowers and a stick, iron nuggets, glass, purple dye and redstone, and a torch;
- a Zoo Workshop, a Stabilized Carrier (Dimensional Preparation research), a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard and a Field Journal;
- a Water Trough and a water bucket, and a Hanging Food Basket.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In Creative, use the spawn egg 3 or 4 times on the flat field. Look at them from all sides, close up. | A blocky moth half a block from wing tip to wing tip, with narrow forewings with sharp, cut-off tips, smaller hindwings behind them, a body of three parts and two thin antennae. Most are Chorus: deep purple forewings with lilac-pink bars and a pale edge, pink hindwings with a dark base, and a purple-grey body striped lilac along its length. Now and then one is Popped: pale lilac forewings with purple bars and pale pink hindwings. Males and females look alike. A fall doesn't hurt them. |
| 2 | Stay in Creative and watch them for 4 or 5 minutes. | They fly in bouts of 2 to 3 minutes, flitting a few blocks at a time about where they were spawned and pausing in the air between, wings a blur. At the end of a bout, with no wall near, one flies off 10 to 16 blocks a few times looking for one, then lands on the ground and rests flat there, wings folded back over each other into an arrowhead, for 40 to 80 seconds. Before it takes off again it shivers: wings raised and buzzing in a short blur for 3 to 5 seconds. It doesn't hover on the spot while it rests. |
| 3 | Build a wall of end stone 4 high and 5 long within a few blocks of them. Watch the next rests. | At the end of a bout each flies to the wall and rests on its side, 2 or 3 blocks up (high first), head up, wings in the arrowhead. Two never rest in the same spot. |
| 4 | Near them, put down 3 single end stone blocks a few blocks apart with a chorus flower on each, and a chorus plant on end stone with a flower on top. Watch a minute or two. | Within about a minute they find the flowers. Each works its way round them: it flies to the nearest one it hasn't just been at, hovers in front of an open side (or just above it), wings a blur, its long proboscis uncoiled into the flower, swaying slowly, for 3 to 8 seconds, then on to the next. It never goes straight back to the one it left, and only one moth is at a flower at a time. |
| 5 | Break one of the flowers while a moth is on its way to it, and watch a minute more. | None hovers at the broken flower; they go on round the others. They never rest on a chorus stalk. |
| 6 | Switch to Survival and walk right up to a resting moth, not sneaking (on the wall at head height, or on the ground at your feet). Then sneak up to another one. | Not sneaking: within about a block and a half it gives a quick shiver (1 second) with a soft flutter, then flies off to rest at least 3 blocks from you. It does no harm. Sneaking, or in Creative, it stays put. |
| 7 | Make Nectar Rations at a crafting table or the Zoo Workshop: 2 sugar and a chorus fruit. | 2 Nectar Rations. (2 sugar and a honeycomb, or sweet berries, still make them too.) |
| 8 | Still in Survival, stand 8 or 9 blocks from one and hold a Nectar Ration. | It flies over and hovers about a block in front of your hands, wings a blur, its proboscis in the jar, with a soft hum, for as long as you hold it. Put it away and it goes back to what it was doing. |
| 9 | Go to the End's outer islands (through an End gateway). | Wild Chorus Moths, 2 to 4 together, among the chorus trees. They go from chorus flower to chorus flower, up the trees too, hovering at each, and rest on an end stone face, never on a chorus stalk. Watch one near an island's edge: it never flies out over the void. |
| 10 | Open the Field Journal on the Chorus Moth and do its tasks. | Tasks: spot one; watch one hover at a chorus flower and sip from it; watch one shiver its wings warm before it flies; see one sip nectar (at a Nectar Feeder, or from a ration you hold). The care notes cover the roofed moth house, chorus flowers, the Nectar Feeder, low light, end stone, and chorus on the floor for the caterpillars. |
| 11 | At the Zoo Workshop make a Nectar Feeder (a glass pane on top, then a cornflower, a stick and a cornflower below) and Purple Nocturnal Lamps (iron nugget, glass, iron nugget; glass, purple dye, glass; iron nugget, redstone, iron nugget). | A Nectar Feeder, and 2 Purple Nocturnal Lamps. |
| 12 | Build a moth house on the flat field: a floor of end stone, end stone brick walls and a full roof (no glass), at least 5 x 5 of floor with exactly 4 blocks of air over it. Inside, put a chorus flower on the floor, a chorus plant with a flower on top, and 2 more chorus plants standing on the floor; a Purple Nocturnal Lamp; the Nectar Feeder with Nectar Rations in it; and a Water Trough beside the floor, filled with water. Measure it with the Habitat Marker and open the Clipboard. | Even empty, it measures as a moth house. The Clipboard shows "Chorus flowers: 2 of 2 (...)", "Nectar Feeders: 1" and "Chorus on the floor: 4 (...)" (and the Soul Moth's lines, while it's empty). |
| 13 | With the Field Journal done, use the Stabilized Carrier on a wild moth with a Nectar Ration in your inventory and release it into the house. Do it for 2 more (a male and a female among them for step 17: the Clipboard shows each one's sex), then add a third chorus flower (one for each moth) and measure again. Stand outside in Creative and watch for a few minutes. | The Clipboard also shows "Low light: yes (no daylight, brightest spot N of 7 allowed): out in bouts at any hour", and there are no care warnings but the enrichment one (none about low floor over the chorus). They're out in bouts at any hour: 2 to 3 minutes flying, then 40 to 80 seconds at rest on a wall (an open block beside it, 1 or more over the floor, high first), wings in the arrowhead. Every flight from rest starts with the 3 to 5 second shiver. Out, they work round the flowers as in step 4, now and then hover at a side of the feeder's dish to sip, 4 to 8 seconds, and flit across the house. None ends up inside a block, on top of another or outside the house. |
| 14 | Switch to Survival, step inside and walk right up to one at rest. | The 1-second shiver, then off to another wall at least 3 blocks from you. |
| 15 | Put a torch inside and measure. Then watch by day (`/time set 6000`) and at night (`/time set 18000`). | The Clipboard says "Low light: no: too bright on N blocks, brightest 14 of 7 allowed (torches, lanterns and glowstone are too bright: light it with red or purple Nocturnal Lamps only): they fly only at night, and comfort can't rise above 50 ...", and a care warning says "Chorus Moths want low light: ...". By day they stay at rest; at night they fly. Take the torch out and measure again: the warning goes. |
| 16 | Break flowers until only 1 is left and measure. | A care warning: "Chorus Moths hover at chorus flowers to sip: 3 or more inside (one for each, 2 at least; 1 now), on end stone or a chorus plant. Comfort can't rise above 50 without them ...". Put them back and measure: the warning goes. |
| 17 | Turn breeding on. Break the 2 chorus plants that have no flower and measure. Then put them back, measure again and skip park days (or wait). | With 2 left: "Needs host plants for the caterpillars: 4 or more chorus plants or flowers standing on the floor (2 now)". With 4, once a pair mates, 2 park days later 3 to 6 caterpillars hatch beside the chorus: grey-purple, the front tapering like a trunk, a black eyespot band edged in lilac behind the head and a short horn at the tail. They crawl along the floor to the foot of a chorus plant and munch it, facing it, then on to another; one to a plant, never two on one block, never at the feeder. Walk up to one in Survival: it pulls its head in and swells its front so the eyespots stare. After 3 park days each burrows into the floor and lies there as a pupa. 2 park days later the moth crawls out, pumps its crumpled wings out for a few seconds, shivers and flies. |
| 18 | Put the Hanging Food Basket in the house and fill it with Nectar Rations. With a keeper assigned, let the feeder and basket run low. | The basket counts as their forage enrichment, and the enrichment warning goes. The keeper refills the Nectar Feeder and the basket with Nectar Rations. |
| 19 | Try a Nectar Ration on a Basic Feeder. Then let a moth out in the wild (conservation release), first on grass, then on end stone. | The Basic Feeder refuses it: "Nectar Rations go in a Nectar Feeder, for Soul Moths and Chorus Moths." The release is refused on grass and allowed on end stone. |
| 20 | Check a Soul Moth, owl, Shimmer Snail or Obsidian Crab exhibit you have. | They behave as before. |
| 21 | Look at the content log after the restart. | No script or content errors. |
