Status words follow the spec's section 0: implemented, partial, missing, unverified. "Unit-tested" means the logic passed Node tests; nothing has been run in the game yet, so every in-game behavior is unverified until `M1_test_plan.md` is done.

## Decisions and assumptions made in M1

- Starter species: rabbit, using the vanilla look (the plan's default). Custom animals get original models from M2.
- Items are crafted at a vanilla crafting table until the Zoo Workshop arrives in M2.
- Recipes not in the spec were proposed here: Park Entrance Gate (planks, gold, iron), Cleaning Rake (sticks, iron nuggets), Exhibit Clipboard (paper, planks, copper), Park Deed (paper, gold), Leafy Ration (leaves + wheat, 4 kelp, or 4 bamboo, each makes 2).
- Catching a rabbit needs one Leafy Ration as bait and a validated exhibit with room, so an animal is never caught with nowhere to go.
- Zoo animals can't be hurt. `/kill` sends the animal to recovery holding instead of deleting it.
- A summoned `zoocraft:zoo_rabbit` without a record is removed.
- Guests pay per in-game minute in small batches rather than once a day, so a new park sees income quickly. Each batch has its own operation id.
- The sidebar shows the park score (0 to 100); the star rating is in the park menu.
- Peaceful care (needs don't drop) is a park setting, off by default.

## Known gaps

- Explosions and pistons that open a fence are caught by a re-check of occupied, loaded exhibits about every 2 minutes, not instantly. Player edits are caught within 2 seconds.
- An animal outside its exhibit is reported, not moved back (spec: never teleport to hide poor pathing). Recovery is by carrier.
- The plugin's `/bedrock-new` wrote the plugin's absolute container path into `package.json`; the scripts were rewritten here without it. Worth fixing in the plugin.
