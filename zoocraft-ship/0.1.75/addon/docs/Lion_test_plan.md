# Lion and big-predator safety test plan (0.1.75)

The first slice of Big predators (Austin's card, 2026-10-15) is secure keeper access and the Lion (spec 5.2: "lion | Lion | Pride rest and scent investigation | Group dens, secure service access"). Lions are dangerous. Inside their exhibit a loose lion warns a player off (crouched, ears flat, a growl), then charges and swipes once for 3 hearts ("Warn, then swipe"). In the wild a pride hunts anyone in Survival or Adventure (Austin: "Also make them very dangerous in the wild"). Keepers never share space with them: every grown lion is shut in a Holding Den (worked from outside the fence) before a keeper goes in through a double Keeper Gate, one gate open at a time. In the zoo a pride rests most of the day in a pile, touching, up on a ledge in the shade; rubs heads and licks; patrols and scent-marks a Scent Post; roars at dusk and before dawn; nurses and plays with its cubs. Lions eat Meat Rations and fast one park day a week. This build adds a new animal, two blocks, an item and textures, so fully close and reopen Minecraft after the build.

Needs: a superflat or test world in Creative with cheats on (switch to Survival where a step says so), a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, a few Reinforced Crates, 2 Keeper Gates, a Basic Feeder, a Water Trough with water, stone bricks, iron ingots, tripwire hooks, logs, string, ferns, raw beef, mutton, porkchop, chicken and rabbit, fence or wall blocks, grass blocks and a few slabs or logs for a ledge. A park day is 20 minutes of play; `/zoocraft:skip 1200` runs one park day now; `/time set` changes the time of day. A savanna is the place to find wild prides. Keep your health up between the danger steps (`/effect @s instant_health`), and turn the difficulty to Easy or harder (lions don't hunt on Peaceful).
