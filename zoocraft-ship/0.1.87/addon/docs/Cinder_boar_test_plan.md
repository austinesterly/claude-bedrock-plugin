# Cinder Boar test plan (0.1.87)

The third slice of the Nether trio (Austin's card, 2026-10-18) is the Cinder Boar (spec 5.3: "cinder_boar | Ash-colored rooting animal | Digs and uses cooling mud pockets | Ash, thermal mud, strong barrier"). It is a fantasy animal sized and behaving like the wild boar. It lives in sounders of sows and piglets and roots in Warm Ash. In the heat of the day it wallows in Thermal Mud and rubs the mud off against a rock. It breaks through wooden fences, and a sow stands up for her piglets. This version adds new entities, a new block and textures, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Thermal Carriers (Heat Systems research), a Thermal Regulator with fuel, a Water Trough, Ember Rations, Warm Ash (8 or more), mud, soul soil and a snowball (for Thermal Mud), stone bricks or nether brick, a few oak fences. A park day is 20 minutes of game time.

| 3 | At the Zoo Workshop (or a crafting table), make Thermal Mud from 4 mud, a soul soil and a snowball. | 4 Thermal Mud: dark blue-grey mud with wet highlights and pale specks. It sounds like mud when you dig it, place it and walk on it. |

Back off and she settles after a few seconds. In Creative, or with the difficulty on Peaceful, nothing happens. |
| 6 | Hold an Ember Ration (or a crimson fungus) and walk up to a sounder. | They let you close and come to you. No guard and no charge. |
| 7 | Walk up to a lone boar in Survival, then hit him. | Within 6 blocks he trots off, and keeps his distance if you follow. Hit him and he charges for real, once for each hit (4 damage, the tusks), then trots off and keeps away. |

| 9 | Release a boar, two sows and a piglet (or let a sow breed).
