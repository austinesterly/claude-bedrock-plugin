# Deep ocean trench, ocean giants and river species: test plan (0.1.124 on)

Module 6b (Austin: "Deep ocean trench, ocean giants and river species", sharks dangerous, "whale sharks are passive",
"big glowing beautiful jelly fish ... they should drift very slowly"). 0.1.124 brings the Whale Shark, the first BIG
FISH: one named animal in a giant tank (not a school), caught one at a time in the Large Water Tank once Marine
Husbandry is researched, fed Fish Flakes from the Aquarium Feeder. New entity, item and texture files: quit Minecraft
fully and start it again before testing. `/zoocraft:build` makes the full zoo with the Whale Shark Tank in it (steps 1
to 3); steps 4 on work in any world with a park.

## 0.1.125: the Great White and the Hammerhead

New entity and texture files again: quit Minecraft fully and start it again. A fresh `/zoocraft:build` has the two shark tanks (steps 12 to 14); the rest works in any world with a park. Do steps 15 and 16 in Survival: the danger is real.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | `/zoocraft:build` in a fresh Superflat world. Walk to the Great White Tank and the Hammerhead Tank. | Two glass-walled tanks of water 2 deep behind a bank (20 x 15 and 22 x 15), an Aquarium Feeder over the water with Fish Rations in it, a Water Filter on the bank. One great white (grey over white, the big triangular fin, the tall tail, 4.5 m) and two hammerheads (bronze-grey, the wide flat head with the eyes on its tips, 4 m) cruise about. |
| 13 | Watch them a minute. | Now and then the great white surges up toward the surface with its jaws open for a few seconds; a hammerhead noses down and swings its head side to side over the floor. The Field Journal's second task for each ("Watch one surge up to the surface", "Watch one sweep the floor") ticks when you watch it. |
| 14 | Open each tank's Exhibit Controller. | "Animals: 1 · needs 256 usable blocks, has 300" (the hammerheads: 2, 320 needed, has 330), an Aquarium Feeder among the equipment, no warning. The keeper line reads "Keeper access: from the bank (keepers fill the Aquarium Feeder from outside the water and never go in)" and the red line "Dangerous: great white sharks circle anyone swimming in their tank, then rush and bite. Keepers fill the Aquarium Feeder from the bank and never go in; nobody should swim in the tank." No Holding Den or Keeper Gate warning. |
| 15 | In Survival, jump into the Great White Tank and swim. | Within a few seconds the action bar says "<name> is warning you off..." and the shark circles you fast, a little below you, head turned in. Stay 3 seconds (or swim right up to it): it rushes you with its jaws open and bites: 3 hearts, a shove, a bite sound; then it backs off and watches. Climb out onto the bank: within half a minute it cruises again. In Creative nothing happens. |
| 16 | Stand on the bank and throw a Fish Ration into the water near the shark. Then use an empty Large Water Tank on it (Marine Husbandry researched). | It stops hunting, swims to the ration and eats it (jaws working, an eating sound). Before the ration: "Too dangerous to tank: throw a Fish Ration into the water near the great white shark and use the Large Water Tank on it while it eats." While it eats: "<name> the Great White Shark is in the tank..." and it is gone from the water. Use the occupied tank on the bank: it is back, swimming free. Without the research: "The Large Water Tank needs Marine Husbandry research at the Research Desk first." |
| 17 | Put Fish Rations and then Meat Rations in an Aquarium Feeder. | Fish Rations go in (the feeder's line says it holds Fish Flakes or Fish Rations). Meat: "An Aquarium Feeder holds Fish Flakes (a dried kelp and wheat seeds make 4) or Fish Rations only." |
| 18 | `/zoocraft:skip 1200` a few times. | The shark tanks' feeders go down by about 3 units a park day (great white) and 4 (two hammerheads); keepers top them up with Fish Rations from a Staff Room, standing on the bank; nobody shifts anything into dens. Welfare stays high. |
| 19 | Find a deep ocean (or `/summon zoocraft:wild_great_white` in deep water) and swim near it in Survival. | It comes straight for you and bites (3 hearts a bite) until you are out of the water or 16 blocks off. Throw a Fish Ration into the water: it goes for the ration and eats, calm for a while; the Large Water Tank takes it then. Hit it: it hunts again. |
| 20 | `/summon zoocraft:wild_hammerhead` in a warm ocean. | The same hunt and calm; wild hammerheads also spawn in ones and threes in warm and lukewarm oceans. The content log shows no AI errors for either shark. |

## 0.1.126: the Goblin Shark and the Anglerfish

New entity and texture files again: quit Minecraft fully and start it again. A fresh `/zoocraft:build` has the two dark tanks (steps 21 to 23); the rest works in any world with a park.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | `/zoocraft:build` in a fresh Superflat world. Walk to the Goblin Shark Tank and the Anglerfish Tank. | Two stone tanks with a tinted glass front and a stone roof, dark inside but for the Nocturnal Lamps hung from the roof. In the big one a pink goblin shark (4 m, the long flat snout over the mouth, small fins, the long low tail) drifts slowly; in the small one a black anglerfish the size of a block hangs in the water, its lure bulb glowing blue-white. |
| 22 | Watch them a minute. | Now and then the goblin shark slings its jaws forward to the tip of its snout and back (the journal's "Watch one shoot its jaws out"); now and then the anglerfish gapes wide with its lure twitching over its mouth ("Watch one gape with its lure twitching"). |
| 23 | Open each tank's Exhibit Controller. | "Dark tank: yes (no daylight on the water, brightest spot 4 of 7 allowed; ...)" and no warning. The goblin shark's: "Animals: 1 · needs 200 usable blocks", the keeper line "from the bank" and the red line "Dangerous: goblin sharks drift up to anyone swimming in their tank, then shoot their jaws out and bite...". |
| 24 | Break a few roof blocks over the goblin shark's water, then measure the tank again with the Habitat Marker (or wait for the next measure). | "Dark tank: no: daylight reaches N of M blocks ...: goblin sharks live in the dark, and in daylight comfort can't rise above 50. Put the roof back and measure: yes again, and comfort recovers. |
| 25 | In Survival, swim in the Goblin Shark Tank. | It warns you off (the action bar) and drifts round you slowly, then rushes with its jaws out and bites (3 hearts): slower than the great white, the same danger. Out of the water it calms. |
| 26 | Throw a Fish Ration into the goblin shark's water and use an empty Large Water Tank on it; then use the Large Water Tank on the anglerfish with nothing thrown. | The goblin shark eats and is taken only then ("Too dangerous to tank..." before). | 27 | Open the Field Journal on each. | The Goblin Shark page: "In the dark: a trench tank roofed over and walled with solid blocks or tinted glass..."; the Anglerfish page the same, ending "Its lure glows, so guests see it even in pitch dark". The second task for each as in step 22. |
| 28 | Find a deep ocean and dive to the floor (or `/summon zoocraft:wild_goblin_shark` and `/summon zoocraft:wild_anglerfish` in deep water). | A wild goblin shark drifting low in the water comes for you, jaws first, until you leave the water or throw a Fish Ration; a wild anglerfish hangs there with its lure lit and never attacks. The content log shows no AI errors for either. |
