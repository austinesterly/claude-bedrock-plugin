# Warped Gecko test plan (0.1.85)

The second slice of the Nether trio (Austin's card, 2026-10-18) is the Warped Gecko (spec 5.3: "warped_gecko | Blue-green luminous climber | Wall clinging and vertical hunting | Registered climbing surfaces and insect feed"). It is a fantasy animal sized and behaving like the New Caledonian giant gecko: it climbs walls and glass, hides in cracks by day, calls at dusk and hunts moths at night. This version adds new entities, a block, a particle and textures, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Thermal Carriers (Heat Systems research), a Thermal Regulator with fuel, a Water Trough, an Insect Hatchery (or Insect Rations from the gift shop), planks, a glass pane and string (for the Insect Feeder), glass, stone or other whole blocks, a Burrow Shelter, warped nylium (a Silk Touch pickaxe in a warped forest). A park day is 20 minutes of game time.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the Nether, `/summon zoocraft:wild_warped_gecko` twice in Creative. Look at them from all sides, then in the dark. | Small blocky geckos, under half a block long, tail and all: a deep teal hide with darker mottling, rows of glowing cyan spots, big amber eyes, pale toe pads. The spots and eyes glow in the dark. A male has a broader head and more, brighter spots. They don't burn in lava or fire. |
| 2 | Find a warped forest (or `/locate biome warped_forest`). | Wild geckos alone or in twos on the warped nylium and up the stems. |
| 3 | At the Zoo Workshop, make an Insect Feeder (string, a glass pane and two planks). | One Insect Feeder: a wooden frame with mesh sides and a lid. |
| 4 | Watch a wild gecko through a day (`/time set 1000`, `12500`, `18000`, `23000`). | By day it sleeps clinging high on a stem (or tucked into a gap if there is one). At dusk it comes out and calls: mouth open, a cluck and then a low growl. At night it climbs about, up, down and across the stems, pausing head down to watch, licking its eyes now and then. Now and then a small moth flutters on a stem near it: it creeps up, freezes, snaps it up and licks its lips. At dawn it goes back up to sleep. |
| 5 | Watch one climb closely. | Its belly lies on the wall in every pose: head up, head down, across with the wall on its left or right. It goes over the top of a block onto it, round outside corners, and off a ledge and down its side. It never passes through a block or hangs under a roof. |
| 6 | In Survival, walk right up to one (within 2 blocks). | It rises on its legs, mouth wide, and hisses; then it runs off up the stem away from you. No damage. In Creative, nothing. |
| 7 | Hold an Insect Ration within 8 blocks of one. | It comes down to the floor and walks to you. An empty Thermal Carrier with an Insect Ration on you lets you pick it up. Put the ration away and it goes back to climbing. |
| 8 | Two wild males near each other at night (summon two, the second without the herd event, or find a pair). | Within 3 blocks of each other they face each other, bob their heads and growl, then one moves off. |
| 9 | Build a terrarium: about 7 x 7 inside, glass walls 5 high, no roof yet. Add a Thermal Regulator with fuel, a filled Water Trough, a Burrow Shelter and an Insect Feeder with Insect Rations. Measure it with the Habitat Marker. | The Clipboard shows "Climbing walls" with the wall faces counted and "roof open" with where the air leads out. The exhibit is unsafe: "Warped Geckos climb glass and walls: the air leads out of the exhibit near ..., so they could climb out." A gecko can't move in. |
| 10 | Roof it over with glass and measure again. | "Climbing walls: N wall faces to cling to, 0 cracks to hide in; roof closed". Geckos can move in. |
| 11 | Make a crack: take one block out of a wall at head height and put a block behind it, so the gap is open only on the inside. Make a second. Measure again. | "2 cracks to hide in". The Clipboard's "Hides" line counts the cracks and the Burrow Shelter. |
| 12 | Release a male, a female and a young one (or skip to a hatchling). Watch through a day. | By day each sleeps in a hide of its own (a crack, or the Burrow Shelter), out of sight; no two share one. At dusk they come out and call. At night they climb the glass and the walls; the male keeps low, within 2 blocks of the floor; the female and the young one go higher. The feeder lets out a moth now and then onto a wall near it, and they hunt it. |
| 13 | At night in Survival, step inside right beside one. | The threat and the hiss, then it runs up the wall. No damage. |
| 14 | At night, hold an Insect Ration inside the terrarium. | They come down to the floor and over to you. Put it away and they climb again. |
| 15 | Take the roof off and wait a night with geckos inside. | A red message: "(name) is climbing out of (exhibit) near x y z!". It goes over the wall and stays outside: the Clipboard says it's outside. Catch it with an empty Thermal Carrier (or tempt it down with an Insect Ration), roof the exhibit and release it back inside. |
| 16 | Put two grown males in one terrarium and leave them a park day. | At night they square up, bobbing and growling. A care warning: "2 grown male warped geckos share the exhibit"; comfort stops at 50. |
| 17 | A terrarium with no crack and no Burrow Shelter for 2 geckos, left a park day. | A care warning that they hide by day (0 hides now for 2); comfort stops at 60. Add a Burrow Shelter each and measure: the warning goes. |
| 18 | Put Insect Rations in a Basic Feeder for geckos only. | "Warped Geckos hunt their food: put an Insect Feeder inside". An Insect Feeder refuses any other ration: "An Insect Feeder holds Insect Rations only". Hedgehogs, raccoons and flamingos can eat from an Insect Feeder too. |
| 19 | Build an empty terrarium with an Insect Feeder in it and measure it. | The Clipboard shows the climbing walls and the roof, so you can check it before any gecko moves in. Without the feeder it asks for one first. |
| 20 | Build the terrarium in the Nether with no light inside and measure it. | "Nocturnal house: yes (dark, and no daylight reaches the Nether ...): warped geckos are up by day for the guests and sleep at night (they glow, so guests see them in the dark too)". In the Overworld under a glass roof it says no: they sleep by day there. |
| 21 | A Viewing Point near the terrarium by day. | A gecko tucked into its crack or the Burrow Shelter isn't counted; one asleep up on a wall is. |
| 22 | Open the Field Journal on the Warped Gecko. | Tasks: spot one, watch one cling to a wall, see one hunt a moth, hear one call at dusk. Care notes: walls to climb, a roof, a hide each, the Insect Feeder, one grown male, the threat and tempting. |
| 23 | Let one out in the wild (conservation release) on netherrack, then on warped nylium. | Refused on netherrack; allowed "on warped nylium". |
| 24 | Turn breeding on with a male and a female and skip park days. | Two hatchlings after 3 park days, a third of a grown one's size. Grown in 6. |
| 25 | Check a hedgehog, an owl and a crimson grazer exhibit you have. | They behave as before. Hedgehogs still use their nocturnal house. |
| 26 | Content log after the restart. | No script or content errors. |
