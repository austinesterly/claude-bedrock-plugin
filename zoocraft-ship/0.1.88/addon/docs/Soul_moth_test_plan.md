# Soul Moth test plan (0.1.88)

The first of the Nether pair is the Soul Moth (spec 5.3: "soul_moth | Pale blue moth | Gathers around soul light | Enclosed airspace, soul lights, nectar"). It is a fantasy hawk moth, drawn big so it's easy to see (Austin's card "Big moth": 0.62 blocks from wing tip to wing tip). It flies at night, circles soul light with its back to the light, sips nectar from a new Nectar Feeder and rests flat on a wall by day. Its young are caterpillars that eat Nether plants, lie in the floor as a pupa, and come out as moths. This version adds new entities, a new block, a new item and textures, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Thermal Carriers (Heat Systems research), a Thermal Regulator with fuel, a Water Trough and a water bucket, sugar, honeycomb or sweet berries, a glass pane, two cornflowers, a stick, stone bricks or blackstone for the walls and roof, a soul lantern, a soul torch, a soul campfire, and 4 or more crimson roots, warped roots, nether sprouts or crimson or warped fungi. A park day is 20 minutes of game time.

| 10 | With the Field Journal done, use a Thermal Carrier on a wild moth with a Nectar Ration in your inventory, and release it into the moth house. On the fifth day a moth comes out on the floor ("It has come out of its pupa as a moth ..."), pumps its crumpled wings out for a few seconds, and flies. |
