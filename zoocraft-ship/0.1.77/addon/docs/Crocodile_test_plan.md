# Crocodile test plan (0.1.77)

The third slice of Big predators (Austin's card, 2026-10-15) is the Crocodile (spec 5.2: "crocodile | Crocodile | Basking and submerged resting | Secure keeper access, shore clearance"). Crocodiles are dangerous like the big cats: inside their exhibit a loose crocodile warns a player off (the threat gape: reared up, puffed, the jaws wide, hissing), then charges and snaps sideways ("Warn, then swipe"), and keepers go in only through the double Keeper Gate with every grown crocodile shut in a Holding Den, and only with the shore kept clear. They bask on a sunny bank by day with their jaws open, float with only the eyes out, rest on the bottom of deep water, and eat about once a week, the biggest first. A female digs a nest in sand near the water, and its warmth sets the hatchlings' sex. In the wild a crocodile lurks in swamp water and lunges out at anyone at the edge, drags them in and rolls (Austin: "Also make them very dangerous in the wild"). This build adds a new animal, a new block and textures, so fully close and reopen Minecraft after the build.

Needs: the 0.1.75 setup (a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Reinforced Crates, 2 Keeper Gates, Holding Dens, a Basic Feeder, Meat Rations), lots of water buckets, sand and some red sand, gravel, grass blocks, a few logs, some flowers or tall grass, fence blocks, slabs or leaves for a roof. A park day is 20 minutes of play; `/zoocraft:skip 1200` runs one park day now; `/time set` changes the time of day; `/weather rain` and `/weather clear` change the weather. Wild crocodiles live in swamps and mangrove swamps, one or two together. Keep your health up between the danger steps (`/effect @s instant_health`), and turn the difficulty to Easy or harder.
