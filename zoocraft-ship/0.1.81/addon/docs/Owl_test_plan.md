# Owl test plan (0.1.81)

The third slice of the Treetop trio (Austin's card, 2026-10-16) is the Owl, a Eurasian eagle-owl (spec 5.2: "owl | Owl | Nocturnal flight and perch watching | Enclosed airspace, nesting boxes"). Owls live in an aviary: a roofed exhibit with at least 4 blocks of air, 2 or more Perches (the new wooden T-perch from the Zoo Workshop) and a Nest Box for eggs. By day an owl roosts on its highest Perch; at dusk it hoots; at night it watches, flies between perches and swoops down to the feeder; at dawn it goes back to roost. It's harmless: get close to one on the ground and it spreads its wings and hisses. The model is the new blocky style. This build adds new animals, a new block and textures, so fully close and reopen Minecraft after the build.

Needs: a Zoo Workshop, a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, Small Carriers, Meat Rations, a Basic Feeder, a Water Trough, a Nest Box, logs and sticks (for Perches), glass or planks for walls and a roof. A park day is 20 minutes of play; `/zoocraft:skip 1200` runs one park day now; `/time set` changes the time of day (0 sunrise, 6000 noon, 12000 sunset, 18000 midnight). Wild owls live in taiga, dark forest and windswept hills, alone or a pair.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/summon zoocraft:wild_owl` a few times in Creative. Look at them from all sides. | Blocky brown owls standing upright, streaked and barred, with orange eyes and two ear tufts. A female is a bit bigger than a male (about 0.7 of a block tall). |
| 2 | At the Zoo Workshop, make Perches (3 logs over 2 sticks in a column). | 2 Perches. Place one on the floor, one on a log, one on a wall top: a wooden post with a bar across. You can walk under the bar's ends; only the bar is solid. |
| 3 | Watch a wild owl near trees through a day (`/time set 6000`, `12500`, `18000`, `23000`). | By day it sits on a treetop with its eyes half shut. At dusk it hoots now and then ("oo-HU", a low flute; a female's is higher, and a pair answer each other). At night it flies from tree to tree, flapping and gliding, and now and then drops to the ground to pounce, then flies back up. Near dawn it settles on a treetop again. |
| 4 | In Survival, walk up to a wild owl on a treetop. | Within about 4 blocks it flies off to another tree (10 or more blocks away). |
| 5 | Find one on the ground (after a pounce) and walk within 3 blocks. | It spreads its wings wide, leans at you, hisses and snaps its bill (no harm), then flies off if you stay. In Creative nothing happens. |
| 6 | Hold a Meat Ration within 12 blocks of a wild owl. | It flies down and lands about 2 blocks from you, facing you, and stays while you hold it. An empty Small Carrier with a Meat Ration on you works the same. Once its Field Journal page is done, use the Small Carrier on it: it goes in. |
| 7 | Build a room about 5 x 5 with glass walls only 3 blocks high, a glass roof, 2 Perches on the floor, a Basic Feeder and a Water Trough, and measure it. Let an owl out of its Small Carrier there. | Refused: "Owls need 4 blocks of air over the floor: raise the roof." Raise the roof so there are 4 blocks of air over the floor and measure again: the Clipboard shows "Perches: 2 (2 or more)". |
| 8 | Take one Perch out, measure, and try to let an owl out there. | Refused: "Owls need 2 or more Perches to sit on (1 now; each needs open air above it)." The Clipboard shows "Perches: 1 (2 or more)" in red. Put it back and measure. |
| 9 | With both Perches on the floor, let two owls out (a female and a male) and open the Clipboard. | "0 blocks between the highest and the lowest (2 or more)", and the warning "The Perches are all about one height: put one 2 or more blocks higher or lower than another" (comfort can't rise above 50). Put one Perch up on a log or a ledge (2 or more blocks higher) and measure: the warning goes. |
| 10 | Add a Basic Feeder with Meat Rations and watch the owls through a day. | By day each roosts on the highest free Perch with its eyes half shut; walk within 5 blocks and its eyes open, and its head turns to follow you. Now and then one preens. At dusk they hoot back and forth. At night they fly from perch to perch through the room's air (never through glass or a wall), flapping off and gliding in, landing feet first on the bar, and now and then swoop down beside the feeder, mantle over the food and eat. Near dawn they go back to roost, and one coughs up a pellet. |
| 11 | In Survival, step into the aviary and stand within 3 blocks of an owl on the floor, a low Perch or in its Nest Box. | The threat display: wings spread, hissing and bill snapping. No harm. One on a high Perch just watches you. |
| 12 | Turn breeding on with no Nest Box in the aviary and skip park days. | Breeding is refused: owls lay their eggs only in a Nest Box. |
| 13 | Place a Nest Box in the aviary and skip park days until eggs come. | The female flies into the Nest Box and sits there ("is sitting on her eggs"). After 3 park days, 2 or 3 owlets: small, fluffy and grey-buff, in the box beside her. |
| 14 | Watch the owlets their first 2 park days. | They stay in the Nest Box, their mother brooding them there by day. At night she flies down to the feeder, eats, and comes back to them; from dusk to dawn they beg at her (wings shivering, a thin hiss). After that they hop and flutter short distances about the floor and onto the low Perches (none more than 2 blocks up). Grown in 6 park days. |
| 15 | Open the Field Journal on the Owl. | Tasks: spot one, hear one hoot, watch one pounce, find a pellet. The wild line (where they live and how to draw one down with a Meat Ration). Care notes: the aviary (4 blocks of air, a roof, 2 Perches at different heights), the Nest Box, their day and night, food. |
| 16 | Check a Void Ray exhibit you have, and a red panda exhibit. | Void Rays still fly and perch only on Void Perches (a wooden Perch isn't a Void Perch for them), and with breeding on they now breed (before, the shelter rule never passed). A red panda pair now has cubs, and so does a pair of hedgehogs, raccoons, Ember Foxes, Lava Rays or Rift Ferrets (before, "the group is full"). |
