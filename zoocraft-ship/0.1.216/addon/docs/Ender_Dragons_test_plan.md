# Ender dragons: test plan (0.1.216)

The game's ender dragon as a zoo mob in a Mob House of its own, caught and kept the zombie's way. It looks like the
game's own (the game's skin and model pieces at the same size, about 16 blocks from wing tip to wing tip; its wings beat,
its jaw opens and shuts, its neck bobs and its tail sways with the beat) and growls now and then. In the zoo an ender
dragon never breathes fire, fires a dragon fireball, perches, knocks anyone about or breaks a block, and no end crystal
heals it; it has no boss bar. It circles round and round under its pen's roof and every 15 to 30 seconds dives down low
over the floor and climbs back up. It never lands and never despawns.

There are no ender dragons outside the End: one waits on the End's main island for whoever comes through the End portal.
It circles the obsidian pillars (the end crystals on top heal it), dives at you and knocks you flying, fires dragon
fireballs that leave clouds of purple breath, and now and then perches on the exit portal in the middle and breathes over
it. Its health is 200 on every difficulty. A kill lights the exit portal home (and lays the dragon egg, the first time);
four end crystals on the portal's sides bring another.

How to catch one (Mob Taming research): fill in the Field Journal first (spot one, then see one calmed: keep your eyes
on it for a few seconds; a zoo one counts). Break the end crystals on the pillars, weaken the dragon to a quarter of its
health or less (50 of 200), then use a Passivity Serum on it up close as it perches on the exit portal. It sparkles
green and calms down. The End sends a new dragon at once: that one is still the one to beat to light the way home.
A calmed one circles low (about 5 blocks over the ground) where it was calmed while someone is within 48 blocks of it,
and hangs in the air when no one is; hold a Meat Ration and it comes over to you. Then, with a Meat Ration in your
inventory, use an empty Mob Cage on it, and use the cage on the floor inside its pen.

What it wants (a pen beside a Mob House, a roof over it, at least 33 by 33 of floor for the first and 400 more blocks
each, 16 blocks of headroom):
- The End's ground (end stone, end stone bricks or obsidian) under half the floor or more. Without it comfort can't rise
  above 55, and the Exhibit Clipboard says why.
- Meat Rations in a Basic Feeder (4 a day); a Water Trough.
- One to a pen: it lives alone and keeps the pen to itself.

They are never bred: catch more.

New mobs: quit Minecraft completely and start it again. Works in any world.
