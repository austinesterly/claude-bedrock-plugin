# Shimmer Snail test plan (0.1.92)

The second of the End trio is the Shimmer Snail (spec 5.4: "shimmer_snail | Pale body, pearlescent spiral shell | Leaves a fading luminous trail | Mineral bed, shade, crevices"). It is built on the giant African land snail and the rock snails, drawn big (about three quarters of a block long crawling), with the glow of Quantula, a snail that lights up. As it crawls it leaves a glowing trail that fades. It rests sealed in its shell in crevices, and pulls into its shell when you come too close. This version adds new entities, textures and a particle, so close Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.

Needs: a superflat world. A Wild Shimmer Snail Spawn Egg, a stack of end stone, an end stone brick slab, a fence, a chorus plant (or chorus flowers to grow one), 12 gravel, 3 nether quartz, bone meal, basalt or blackstone, a Zoo Workshop or a crafting table, a Stabilized Carrier (Dimensional Preparation research), a Habitat Marker, an Exhibit Clipboard, a Field Journal, a Basic Feeder, a Water Trough and a water bucket, glass for the walls and a few blocks of roof, and a stack of end stone bricks for the crevices.

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In Creative, use the spawn egg 3 or 4 times on the flat field. Look at them from all sides, close up, then at night or under a roof. | A blocky snail about three quarters of a block long. It has a round coiled shell with a spiral painted on both sides, its mouth low at the front where the body comes out, and the coil's centre a little to its right. On its head are two eye stalks pointing up and two short tentacles. Most are Pearl (an ivory body, a pearl-white shell with a pink and aqua sheen round the whorls). Now and then one is Opal (a pale lilac body, a lavender shell with a gold and teal sheen). In the dark the front of its foot, under its head, glows green-aqua, and the shell's sheen glows faintly. |
| 2 | Stay in Creative and watch one for 3 or 4 minutes. | It crawls slowly, about a block every 5 seconds, turning gently. Behind it lies a glowing green-aqua trail: small flat marks along its way that fade over about 45 seconds. It stops to look about, its front raised and its eye stalks waving. Every 1.5 to 2.5 minutes it pulls into its shell and seals the mouth with a white seal for 40 to 80 seconds, right where it is on the flat field. Then it comes out and crawls on. |
| 3 | Watch two or three of them together for a few minutes. | Now and then one crawls along another's fresh trail with its head low. When it catches up, the two touch tentacles for a moment. One that comes up to another standing still waits a moment, then goes round it. They never end up on top of each other. |
| 4 | Put down some end stone near them: single blocks, a stack of 2, the slab, the fence and the chorus plant. | A snail crawls up the face of a single block, front raised, and over the top. It eases down off the far side. It never climbs the stack of 2, and never gets onto the slab, the fence top or the chorus plant. |
| 5 | Switch to Survival and walk up to one, not sneaking. | Within 3 blocks it pulls into its shell for 4 to 8 seconds, then comes out. Back off a few blocks, wait till it's out, then walk up again. Each time it comes out sooner. After 4 times it stops pulling in for you. Leave it alone for a minute and walk up again: it pulls in as at first. |
| 6 | Sneak up to another one. Then stand next to it and punch it. | Sneaking, it lets you come right up. The punch makes it pull in. |
| 7 | Make Mineral Pellets (basalt or blackstone with bone meal makes 2). Stand 4 or 5 blocks from one and hold them. | It turns and crawls over to a block from you, then reaches up at the pellets with its front raised, for as long as you hold them. It doesn't pull in while you hold them. Put them away and it goes back to what it was doing. |
| 8 | Go to the End's outer islands (through an End gateway). | Wild Shimmer Snails, 2 to 4 together, on the end stone. They crawl about leaving their trails, graze the end stone with their heads down (a dim glow under the head), and rest sealed in their shells in crevices at the foot of rocks or against them. Herd one toward an island's edge: it never crawls off into the void. |
| 9 | Open the Field Journal on the Shimmer Snail and do its tasks. | Tasks: spot one; watch one crawl, leaving its glowing trail; come close to one in Survival and watch it pull into its shell; find one resting, sealed in its shell (a white seal over the mouth). The care notes cover crevices (how to make one), Mineral Substrate, Mineral Pellets, their bouts, the trail and breeding. |
| 10 | Make Mineral Substrate: 4 gravel, a nether quartz and bone meal make 4. Do it 3 times. | 12 Mineral Substrate. |
| 11 | Build an exhibit for 3 snails: glass walls, a floor of at least 15 blocks (9 for one, 3 more for each extra), 2 blocks of air over it, and a roof over at least a quarter of the floor for shade. Put 4 or more end stone and 4 or more Mineral Substrate in the floor, plus a Basic Feeder with Mineral Pellets and a Water Trough with water. Make 3 crevices along one wall: build that wall of end stone bricks 2 blocks thick and at least 2 high, and leave a block out at its foot on the inside, 3 times, a block apart. Measure it with the Habitat Marker and open the Clipboard. | "Crevices: 3 of 3 (one for each snail to rest in: a one-block gap at the foot of a wall, roofed over and walled on three sides, open onto the floor)" and "Mineral Substrate: 4 of 4 blocks to graze and lay their eggs in" (or more). No care warnings about crevices or the substrate. |
| 12 | With the Field Journal done, use the Stabilized Carrier on a wild snail with Mineral Pellets in your inventory. Release it in the exhibit. Do the same for 2 more, then stand outside in Creative and watch for a few minutes. | They crawl about the floor, never up onto the feeder or the trough. They graze the Mineral Substrate (dim marks under the head), eat at the feeder, look about, and follow each other's trails, touching tentacles when one catches up. At the end of a bout each crawls into a crevice head first and seals itself in. Next time it goes back to the same crevice while that one is free. Two never rest in one crevice, and they never end up on top of each other. |
| 13 | Switch to Survival, step inside and walk up to one. | Within 2 blocks it pulls into its shell, the same way as in step 5. |
| 14 | Fill one crevice with a block and measure again. Then dig out Mineral Substrate until there are 3 in the floor, and measure. | With 2 crevices: a care warning, "Shimmer Snails rest sealed in a crevice, one to each: 3 or more (2 now; comfort can't rise above 50 without them) ...", and the snail without one rests out on the floor instead. With 3 substrate: "Shimmer Snails graze Mineral Substrate, rasping at it: at least 4 blocks in the floor (3 now ...". Put both back and measure: the warnings go, and they use the crevice again. |
| 15 | Turn breeding on and wait (or skip park days). | The Clipboard shows each one as a hermaphrodite. Two pair up and one lays 2 or 3 eggs in the Mineral Substrate. In 2 park days the hatchlings come up on the substrate. They are about a third the size, glow, and leave a fainter trail. They crawl after their parent, keeping close, and seal themselves in beside it when it rests. In 4 park days they're grown. |
| 16 | Check an End Hopper, Chorus Deer, Void Ray or Rift Ferret exhibit you have, and a moth or beetle exhibit. | They behave as before. |
| 17 | Look at the content log after the restart. | No script or content errors. |
