# 0.1.108: the crocodile, tiger, lion, raccoon and zebra redrawn simply

Austin (2026-10-26): "We also need to undo the super detailed animals in this mod (Croc, Tiger, Lion, Raccoon, Zebra)". The five are redrawn in the simple vanilla style: the same skeleton, sizes and animations, far fewer boxes and one texel a unit. A full restart of Minecraft (the skins are new sizes), then:

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|

Same sizes as before. |
| 2 | Watch each walk, eat, sleep and do its special moves (the lion's swipe, the tiger's stalk, the crocodile's float and lie-out, the raccoon's wash, the zebra's kick). | Every animation plays as before: no part floats off, stretches or vanishes. |
| 3 | Look at every coat: crocodile adults and young, Bengal and Amur tigers, lion, dark lion, lioness and cub, grey and cinnamon raccoons, Burchell's and Grant's zebras and a foal. | Each is telling apart at a glance; the cub is spotted, the Grant's zebra has no brown shadow stripes, the Amur tiger is paler. |
