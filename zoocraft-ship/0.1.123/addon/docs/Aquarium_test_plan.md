# Aquariums and the first four fish: test plan (0.1.119)

Austin: "Add fish species so zoos can have an aquarium. Players can gather fish in large numbers, and fish need less
watching than the animals." 0.1.119 brings Cod, Salmon, Tropical Fish and Pufferfish as zoo animals kept in schools:
a Water Transport Tank catches a whole shoal at once, an aquarium is an exhibit with a tank in it measured from the
bank, an Aquarium Feeder over the water holds Fish Flakes for them, and at most 6 of a school swim about as mobs (the
rest are counted). New entity, block, item and texture files: quit Minecraft fully and start it again before testing.
`/zoocraft:build` makes the full zoo with four tanks in it (steps 1 to 4); steps 5 on work in any world with a park.
